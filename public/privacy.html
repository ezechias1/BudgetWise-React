Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

BudgetWise ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use BudgetWise:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password (or Google sign-in credentials)
  • Financial data: Income, expenses, savings goals, and budget categories that you manually enter, across your Personal, Business and Family modes
  • Trips and business cards: Trip names and date ranges, and which of your accounts or cards you have marked as business, so trip spending can be separated from personal spending
  • Family and Junior data: Family member profiles you create, and — for a child profile — the information described in section 8 below
  • Bank account details you enter manually: The bank name, account nickname, account type and balance you type in yourself. Automatic bank syncing is not currently active (see section 4)
  • Notification subscriptions: If you enable push notifications, the browser-issued push endpoint and keys needed to deliver them
  • Device information: Browser type, device type, and operating system for app functionality
  • Usage data: Features used and pages visited within the app (see section 7)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the BudgetWise service
  • To display your financial data, charts, and insights
  • To send budget alerts and notifications, and to notify a parent when a child completes a chore or requests money (with your permission)
  • To show a child age-appropriate Junior lessons, based on the date of birth their parent entered
  • To measure aggregate usage and page performance so we can improve the app and fix bugs (see section 7)

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with encryption at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Row-level security on database tables
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
  • Junior PINs are stored as a hash, never as the digits themselves
  • We never ask for or store your online banking username or password

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database, authentication and server functions
  • Vercel: Hosting, plus Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights for aggregate usage and performance measurement
  • Google: OAuth sign-in (optional), and Google Analytics on our public policy and marketing pages

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy and security standards.

Automatic bank syncing is not currently active. Bank accounts and business cards in the app are those you add manually. We do not currently transmit your data to any bank-linking provider. If we enable automatic bank syncing in future, we will name the provider here and update this policy before it goes live.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with the third-party services listed above as necessary to provide the BudgetWise service.

6. Your Rights (POPIA Compliance)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and other applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent for notifications at any time
  • Object to processing of your personal information

To exercise any of these rights, use the "Delete All My Data" option in your account settings or contact us at ezechiasmulamba@gmail.com.

7. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

BudgetWise uses browser local storage to save your preferences (theme, currency, account mode, and whether you have completed the intro tour). Your session is kept signed in using storage set by Supabase Auth.

We use analytics to understand aggregate usage and performance:

  • Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights in the app, which record page views and page-load performance
  • Google Analytics on our public policy and marketing pages

We do not use these to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell what they collect. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

8. Children's Privacy (BudgetWise Junior)

BudgetWise Junior is a feature intended for children, and it is set up and controlled by a parent or guardian. A child cannot sign themselves up: a Junior profile exists only inside a parent's Family account, and only a parent can create one. Junior lessons are grouped into age brackets covering approximately ages 7 to 17.

When a parent creates a Junior profile, we collect and store:

  • The child's name or nickname, as entered by the parent
  • The child's date of birth, used to select age-appropriate lessons
  • A 4-digit PIN chosen for the child, stored as a hash
  • The child's activity within Junior: chores marked as done, lesson progress and streaks, their Save / Spend / Give split, savings goals they propose, money they request from a parent, and the record of what a parent approved, adjusted, declined or paid

We do not ask a child for an email address or phone number, and Junior has no messaging, no social features, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. A child signing in with their PIN sees only their own Junior view; they cannot see the parent's accounts, expenses or bank information.

A parent can at any time view everything stored for their child, reset the child's PIN, or remove the child's profile — which deletes that child's Junior data, including their chore, lesson and request history.

If you believe a child's information has been entered without the involvement of a parent or guardian, contact us at ezechiasmulamba@gmail.com and we will delete it.
