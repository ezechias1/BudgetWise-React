1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

BudgetWise is a smart budgeting Progressive Web App (PWA) that helps individuals,
businesses, and families take control of their finances. It offers expense tracking,
savings goals, AI-powered financial advice, bank sync, currency conversion, and
multi-account management — all from one app.

The app operates on a freemium subscription model. Users get core features for free
and unlock premium features (bank sync, business/family modes, advanced reports)
with a Pro subscription.

BudgetWise is built for a global audience with special focus on South Africa and
emerging markets where budgeting tools are limited and financial literacy is growing.


2. WHAT BUDGETWISE DOES
================================================================================

CORE FEATURES (FREE):
- Manual expense tracking with 12+ categories
- Visual spending breakdowns (pie charts, bar charts, trend lines)
- Monthly budget overview with income vs. spending analysis
- Savings goals with progress tracking (up to 3)
- Currency converter with real-time exchange rates (15+ currencies)
- AI-powered financial advice and insights
- Tithe calculator (auto 10% of income, optional)
- Recurring expense detection
- Dark and light themes
- Installable PWA (works offline, feels like a native app)
- Offline mode — queue actions when disconnected, auto-sync when back online

SOUTH AFRICA-SPECIFIC FEATURES (FREE for SA users):
- Load Shedding Tracker — track the real cost of power outages (generator
  fuel, UPS/inverter, candles, spoiled food, mobile data, eating out)
  Expenses with "Load Shedding" category auto-populate the tracker.
- Stokvel Group Savings — create or join stokvel groups with invite codes.
  Features: rotating payout schedule, member contribution tracking (who paid
  / who didn't), admin approval for new members, bank reference for EFT
  payments, contribution reminders (1st and 25th of each month), start/end
  dates, contribution history, and admin-confirmed payments.
- SA expense categories: Airtime & Data, Taxi / Minibus, Load Shedding,
  Stokvel Contribution, School Fees
- ZAR auto-default for South African users
- Country picker at signup with timezone auto-detection
- SMS/USSD expense tracking (coming soon — Africa's Talking integration)

PRO FEATURES (PAID):
- Bank sync — automatic transaction import from linked bank accounts
  (Stitch for SA, Plaid for USA, Salt Edge for global)
- Auto transaction routing between personal/business/family accounts
- Push notifications — budget alerts (80%, 90%, 100%), inactivity reminders
- Business mode — invoices, clients, P&L statements, tax reports
- Family mode — shared budgets, allowances, chores & rewards, family goals
- BudgetWise Junior — teach kids (ages 10-13) to budget from inside the
  same app. Parents assign chores and lessons with rand rewards; kids get
  their own PIN login and kid-friendly UI with Save/Spend/Give jars, a
  savings goal, and daily money lessons sourced from financial-literacy
  curricula. Parent approves every chore and settles up in cash or EFT —
  BudgetWise tracks who owes what, never moves money. See Section 11 for
  roadmap. (Launching Q2 2026 as part of existing Pro tier.)
- Unlimited savings goals
- Export reports (CSV & PDF)
- Priority support


3. HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

USER JOURNEY:

Step 1: Sign Up
   - User creates an account (email or Google sign-in)
   - Chooses account type: Personal (free), Business (Pro), or Family (Pro)
   - Sets currency, monthly income, and savings goal

Step 2: Track Expenses
   - Add expenses manually via form or quick-add bar
   - Categorize spending (Housing, Food, Transport, Tithe, etc.)
   - View spending breakdowns by category, daily, and monthly

Step 3: Set Goals
   - Create savings goals with targets and deadlines
   - Track progress with visual gauges
   - Get notified when goals are reached

Step 4: Connect Bank (Pro)
   - Link bank account via Plaid (USA) or Salt Edge (SA, Europe, Global)
   - Transactions auto-import and categorize
   - Real-time balance and spending alerts

Step 5: Get Insights
   - AI analyzes spending patterns
   - Personalized advice on saving more
   - Budget warnings before overspending

TECHNOLOGY STACK:
- Frontend: Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3 (no frameworks — fast & lightweight)
- Backend: Supabase (PostgreSQL database + authentication + edge functions)
- Charts: Chart.js
- Payments: Stripe
- Bank Sync: Plaid (USA), Salt Edge (SA, Europe, 50+ countries)
- Hosting: Vercel / Netlify (free tier)
- Push Notifications: Web Push API via Service Worker

6. COSTS & EXPENSES
================================================================================

A. STRIPE PAYMENT PROCESSING FEES
--------------------------------------------------------------
Stripe charges 2.9% + R5.00 per successful transaction.

Per Pro Monthly subscriber (R79):
  - Stripe fee: R79 x 2.9% + R5 = R7.29
  - Net revenue: R71.71

Per Pro Yearly subscriber (R659):
  - Stripe fee: R659 x 2.9% + R5 = R24.11
  - Net revenue: R634.89

At 400 Pro users (Month 12, Conservative):
  - 280 monthly: 280 x R7.29     = R2,041 / month
  - 10 yearly transactions/mo: 10 x R24.11 = R241 / month
  - TOTAL STRIPE FEES             = ~R2,282 / month

At 2,000 Pro users (Year 2):
  - TOTAL STRIPE FEES             = ~R11,400 / month

At 10,000 Pro users (Year 3):
  - TOTAL STRIPE FEES             = ~R57,000 / month


B. SALT EDGE (BANK SYNC - SA & GLOBAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Salt Edge pricing is per active bank connection (user linked to a bank).

Estimated pricing tiers:
  - Sandbox/Development:          FREE (fake data, unlimited)
  - Startup (up to 100 connections): ~R2,000 - R4,000 / month
  - Growth (up to 1,000 connections): ~R6,000 - R12,000 / month
  - Scale (5,000+ connections):   ~R20,000 - R40,000 / month
  - Enterprise (10,000+):         Custom pricing

Not all Pro users will link a bank. Estimated 40% bank link rate:

At 400 Pro users -> 160 bank connections:
  - SALT EDGE COST                = ~R4,000 / month

At 2,000 Pro users -> 800 bank connections:
  - SALT EDGE COST                = ~R10,000 / month

At 10,000 Pro users -> 4,000 bank connections:
  - SALT EDGE COST                = ~R35,000 / month

10. COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
================================================================================

- Multi-mode accounts: Personal, Business, and Family in one app
- South African focus: ZAR currency, SA bank integration, local context
- SA-specific features NO competitor has:
    * Load shedding cost tracker (generator fuel, UPS, spoiled food)
    * Stokvel group savings with rotating payouts and invite codes
    * SA expense categories (airtime, taxi, school fees)
    * SMS/USSD expense tracking (coming soon)
- BudgetWise Junior (coming Q2 2026): the only adult budgeting app in SA
  that doubles as a kids' financial-literacy tool. Parents already paying
  for Pro get a full earn-save-spend-give platform for their children, at
  no extra cost. Direct competition (Greenlight, GoHenry) charges $5-10/mo
  per child and requires a debit card; we teach the same habits with no
  card, no banking partnership, and included in the existing Pro tier.
- Global reach: 15+ currencies, 50+ countries via Salt Edge
- Lightweight PWA: No app store required, works offline, installs instantly
- Offline-first: Queue actions offline, auto-sync when reconnected
- AI-powered insights: Smart advice based on spending patterns
- Tithe tracking: Built-in 10% tithe calculator (unique feature)
- Freemium model: Low barrier to entry, upgrade when ready
- Free trial: 1 month Pro free, no credit card required

COMPETITORS & DIFFERENTIATION:
- 22seven (SA): Bank-focused, no stokvel, no load shedding, shutting down
- YNAB: US-focused, expensive ($14.99/mo), no SA features
- Wallet by BudgetBakers: No SA bank support, no stokvel
- Mint: US-only, shutting down (migrating to Credit Karma)
- StokvelApp: Stokvel-only, no full budgeting
- BudgetWise advantage: All-in-one (budgeting + stokvel + load shedding
  + bank sync + business/family modes) at R79/month
