## Product Hunt Description

BudgetWise is a free, full-featured budgeting PWA with features built specifically for South African users — load shedding cost tracking, stokvel group savings, and SA bank categories — plus universal features like receipt OCR scanning, multi-currency support, and 3 account modes (Personal, Business, Family).

**Key features:**
- Track expenses with 18+ categories, recurring detection, and budget limits
- Savings goals with progress bars and smart funding advice
- Receipt scanner (OCR) — snap a photo, auto-fill the expense
- Load Shedding Tracker — know the real cost of power outages
- Stokvel Groups — create/join with invite codes, track contributions
- Business mode — invoices, clients, P&L reports, tax estimates
- Family mode — allowances, chores with rewards, wish lists, spending tracker
- Multi-bank account management with primary account selection
- 150+ currencies with live exchange rates
- Dark/light themes, works offline, installs as a PWA

**Tech:** React + TypeScript, Supabase (auth + database), Chart.js, Tesseract.js (OCR), Capacitor (Android)

**Free forever** — Pro features unlock business, family, and bank sync modes.
