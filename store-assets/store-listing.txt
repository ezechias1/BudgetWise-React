APP NAME: BudgetWise - Smart Budget Tracker

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars):
Track expenses, stokvels, load shedding costs & savings — built for South Africa

FULL DESCRIPTION:
--------------------------------------------------------------
BudgetWise is your all-in-one budgeting app that makes managing money simple,
visual, and stress-free.

Whether you're tracking personal spending, running a business, or managing a
family budget — BudgetWise has you covered.

KEY FEATURES:

TRACK EVERY RAND
- Add expenses in seconds with quick-add or detailed forms
- 12+ spending categories with smart icons
- Recurring expense detection
- Visual pie charts and spending breakdowns

SET & CRUSH SAVINGS GOALS
- Create savings goals with targets and deadlines
- Track progress with beautiful visual gauges
- Celebrate with confetti when you hit your goal

SMART BUDGET INSIGHTS
- See exactly where your money goes each month
- AI-powered financial advice tailored to your spending
- Budget warnings at 80%, 90%, and 100% of income
- 6-month spending trend analysis

MULTI-CURRENCY SUPPORT
- 15+ currencies including ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP, NGN
- Real-time exchange rates
- Built for South Africa and the world

BUILT FOR SOUTH AFRICA
- Load Shedding Tracker — know the real cost of power outages
  (generator fuel, UPS, candles, spoiled food, mobile data)
- Stokvel Groups — create or join stokvels with invite codes, track
  contributions, rotating payouts, see who paid and who didn't
- SA expense categories: Airtime & Data, Taxi, Load Shedding, Stokvel
- ZAR auto-default, SA bank support (Stitch integration)
- Works offline — save expenses without internet, syncs when connected

TITHE TRACKER
- Optional 10% tithe auto-calculated from your income
- Toggle on/off — no judgment, just convenience

3 ACCOUNT MODES:
- Personal: Track your own spending and savings
- Business (Pro): Invoices, clients, P&L, tax reports
- Family (Pro): Shared budgets, allowances, chores & rewards

BUDGETWISE JUNIOR (Pro — coming soon):
- Teach your kids (ages 10-13) to budget from inside the same app
- Parents assign chores with rand rewards, approve completed ones
- Kids get their own PIN login and kid-friendly home screen
- Real IOU ledger — "You owe Sarah R85" — no fake money, no forgotten allowance
- Save / Spend / Give jars teach saving habits at point of payment
- Daily 3-minute money lessons sourced from leading financial-literacy curricula
- Multi-child support; each kid has their own goal, streak, and progress
- Included in existing Pro subscription — no extra fee

BANK SYNC (Pro):
- Connect your bank account for automatic transaction import
- Supports banks in USA, South Africa, Europe, and 50+ countries
- Real-time balance updates

WORKS EVERYWHERE:
- Progressive Web App — installs on any device
- Works offline
- Dark and light themes
- Mobile-first design

FREE TO START:
- Core features are 100% free, forever
- Upgrade to Pro for bank sync, business/family modes, and more
- Try Pro free for 1 month — no credit card required

Download BudgetWise today and start your journey to financial freedom.
